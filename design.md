# Context
You are the only front end developer on the team for the company "SafeTrail". SafeTrail is a student-centered cyberbullying reporting and case management platform for Philippine higher education institutions. It enables students to confidentially report harassment, securely preserve digital evidence, track case progress, and access support resources. University administrators can review reports, verify evidence authenticity, manage cases, and document interventions through a dedicated dashboard.

# Objective
Develop a web application focused on cyberbullying incident reporting, secure digital evidence preservation, and case management. The website should allow students to submit reports, upload evidence, and monitor case progress, while enabling university administrators to review, verify, and manage reported incidents efficiently.
